Privacy Policy

Floatify Dialer (Android, package com.floatify.dialer) · Last updated: 3 November 2026

In short:

Floatify Dialer has no account, no server of its own and no analytics. Your contacts, call history and calls stay on your phone. The developer does not receive them.

The only data that leaves your phone is what the Google AdMob advertising banner needs to show ads, and only after the consent choice described below.

1. Who is responsible

Floatify Dialer is made by an independent developer in the Netherlands. For questions about this policy, see Contact.

2. What the app does with data on your phone

Floatify Dialer is a calling app. To do its job it uses the following information, which it reads and processes only on your device:

DataWhy
Contacts (names, numbers, photos, email, company, notes)To show who is calling, to let you search and call contacts, and to save the changes you make (such as adding a number or a photo or ticking WhatsApp or Teams on a contact). Changes are written to the contacts on your phone.
Call logTo show your recent calls (missed, incoming, outgoing) and to add the calls you make with the app.
Phone state, phone numbers and callsTo receive, place, answer, decline and end calls, to choose a SIM, and to route the audio to the phone speaker, earpiece or a connected Bluetooth device.
Names of paired Bluetooth devicesTo offer them as an answer option, such as headphones or a car.
Microphone permissionNeeded by Android so that a calling app can carry a call. The app does not record audio and does not store call audio.
Notification access (optional)Only if you switch it on. The app then recognises incoming calls from apps such as WhatsApp, Teams, Signal or Telegram by looking at their call notification, so you can answer or decline with the buttons shown. For WhatsApp it can also keep a contact's profile picture from a notification, so a contact without a photo can show it. This stays on your phone. Other notifications are ignored.
Display over other appsTo show the floating answer and decline buttons on top of other apps. No data is read.
Your settingsStored locally in the app (for example your hang-up and ad preferences).

None of this information is sent to the developer or to any server operated by the developer. The app does not sell your data and does not share it with third parties, apart from the advertising described next.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Floatify Dialer is free and shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the Recents and Contacts screens. It is never shown on the keypad or during a call. The ads are provided by Google AdMob, which is part of Google.

To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and use data such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Google uses this to deliver ads, to limit and measure them, to prevent fraud and, if you allow it, to personalise them. The app does not give AdMob access to your contacts, call log or notifications.

Consent. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland) the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before it starts the ad SDK. You can change this choice later if the form offers that option in the app. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

How Google handles this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Backups

The app allows Android's standard backup. If you have Google backup switched on, your app settings may be included in your own Google backup under your own Google account. The developer has no access to it.

5. Sharing and retention

The developer does not collect personal data, so there is nothing for the developer to share, keep or delete. Data in the app stays on your phone until you remove it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's own settings and stored profile pictures. Contacts and the call log belong to Android and remain unless you delete them there.

6. Your rights

Because the developer does not receive your personal data, there is nothing to request from the developer. You control the data on your phone: you can revoke each permission at any time in Android settings, switch off notification access, or uninstall the app. For data processed by Google for advertising you can use the controls mentioned above and in Google's privacy policy. If you live in the EU or UK you have the rights described in the GDPR, including access, correction, deletion and the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

7. Children

Floatify Dialer is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security

The app keeps its data in Android's private app storage and in Android's contacts and call log, and uses only the permissions listed above. Ad requests are made by the Google SDK.

9. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated and the date at the top will change. The current version is always available at this address.
